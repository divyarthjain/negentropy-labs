Product

Ciel: a robot brain that fits on a Raspberry Pi In development

Most robots are told what to do. Ciel learns how its world behaves by acting in it, and keeps learning when the world changes.

01

Learns on the device

A small world model runs on the robot and learns from its own experience. No cloud round-trip.

02

Adapts to change

New floor, heavier load, worn motor: Ciel notices its predictions are off and re-learns in a few tries.

03

Low-cost hardware

Built for hobby-grade servos, a Pi and a camera, so labs, schools and makers can actually afford it.

Why "negentropy"

Machines that make order out of chaos

Left alone, everything drifts toward disorder. Physicists call that entropy. A robot is a small engine that pushes the other way: it sorts, builds, carries and repairs. That push is negentropy, and it is what we build for.
